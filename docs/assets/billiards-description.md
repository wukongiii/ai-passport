# Doubao Billiards Scorekeeper

Turn your pocket device into a billiards scorekeeper. Use three buttons to adjust scores, or tell your AI assistant what to change. Chinese eight-ball supports two players, undo, custom player names, and spoken scores. Open the phone setup page to configure Wi-Fi and an AI model. Manual scoring works offline; AI voice control requires Wi-Fi and a compatible model.

[Watch the device demo (about 80 seconds)](https://github.com/wukongiii/ai-passport/blob/feature/billiards-scorekeeper/assets/videos/doubao-billiards-demo.mp4)

Chinese eight-ball is ready to play. Chase and Snooker are UI previews without scoring yet. The cover is an AI-generated promotional illustration; the demo shows the real device.
